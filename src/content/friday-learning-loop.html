Brain Smart Start

Time: 15 minutes. Open the Brain Smart Start Rebus game and work through the newest additions with your team: Open the Brain Smart Start Rebus

How to Play Together

  • Use one shared screen unless your teacher directs otherwise.
  • Rotate who speaks first. Give everyone quiet thinking time before calling an answer.
  • Before moving on, explain how the placement, size, repetition, direction, or image creates the answer.
  • If your team is stuck, request one hint before revealing the solution.
  • Stop at 15 minutes even if puzzles remain. The goal is shared reasoning, not speed alone.
